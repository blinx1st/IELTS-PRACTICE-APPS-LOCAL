Adapting to the effects of climate change
A.
All around the world, nations are already preparing for, and adapting to,
climate change and its impacts. Even if we stopped all CO2 emissions
tomorrow, we would continue to see the impact of the CO2 already released
since industrial times, with scientists forecasting that global warming would
continue for around 40 years. In the meantime, ice caps would continue to
melt and sea levels rise. Some countries and regions will suffer more extreme
impacts from these changes than others. It's in these places that innovation is
thriving.
